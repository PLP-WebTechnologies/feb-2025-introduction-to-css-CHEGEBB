Lush Forests

Forests cover approximately 31% of our planet's land surface. They are the lungs of our Earth, producing oxygen and absorbing carbon dioxide. From the dense rainforests of the Amazon to the coniferous forests of the north, these ecosystems host incredible biodiversity.

Majestic Oceans

Oceans cover more than 70% of Earth's surface and contain 97% of Earth's water. They are home to countless species, from microscopic plankton to the enormous blue whale. The depths of our oceans remain largely unexplored, with new species being discovered regularly.

Towering Mountains

Mountains make up about one-fifth of the world's landscape. These magnificent formations create unique ecosystems at different altitudes. From the Himalayas to the Andes, mountains have inspired awe and reverence throughout human history.
